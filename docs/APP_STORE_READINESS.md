# App-store readiness

Current status: prerelease engineering candidate, **not store-ready**.

Implemented evidence includes an explicit 18+ product, report/block/deletion controls,
public account-deletion instructions, draft privacy/terms, permission-purpose strings,
no microphone request, no payment claims, no background location, and configuration
that fails closed for protected builds.

Owner gates:

- [ ] Legal approval and versioned Terms/Privacy; retention and appeal policy
- [ ] Permanent support/security/moderation contacts and response coverage
- [ ] Hosted privacy, support, and deletion URLs
- [ ] Production Supabase/EAS/project identity and campus authorization
- [ ] App Store/Play metadata, screenshots, age/content/data-safety questionnaires
- [ ] Physical-device accessibility, email, push, QR, deletion, and recovery evidence
- [ ] Apple/Google signing, TestFlight/internal-track review, reviewer test account plan
- [ ] Final legacy bundle/package decision and privacy manifest/data declarations

Do not claim attorney approval, campus partnership, production readiness, or store
acceptance until those gates are evidenced.
