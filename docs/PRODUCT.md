# Product specification

Ruckus helps an 18+ verified student answer “what can I do with people on campus?” A
right swipe or Join calls the transactional RSVP operation. The outcome is confirmed,
pending host approval, or ordered waitlist; only confirmed attendees and scoped hosts
receive the private event chat.

## Primary journeys

1. Public visitor understands the product and opens a safe event/organization/referral
   page without seeing attendee, chat, private report, or exact-coordinate data.
2. Student verifies a campus-domain email, attests age/safety, completes a profile, and
   discovers future eligible events.
3. Student joins, receives a truthful capacity state, manages the RSVP in My Events,
   chats only when confirmed, adds a calendar entry explicitly, and checks in with a
   rotating host QR.
4. Authorized host creates and previews an event, manages attendee decisions,
   announcements, QR rotation, cancellation, sharing, and archive state.
5. Organization owner invites scoped members, changes roles with audit history, and
   submits verification or claim evidence for review.
6. Student can report/block, control privacy/notifications, request data, and request
   deletion. Admins use a server-authorized moderation queue.

Ruckus Crews remains an optional small-group activity subsystem. No payments, direct
messages, anonymous chat, superficial recurring-event UI, or live location tracking are
included. Legal documents are drafts pending owner/counsel review.

## Beta limitations

Cover-media and organization-logo storage exist with RLS, but final upload/editor QA is
not a release claim. Data-export requests are idempotently queued; a hosted artifact
generator and delivery policy require owner infrastructure. App-store metadata,
support contact, legal URLs, production campus/project, and physical-device acceptance
remain external gates.
